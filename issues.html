Issue Categories We Detect

Our AI model is trained to identify and categorize various neighborhood issues

💧

Water & Drainage Issues

Blocked drains, water leaks, flooding, sewage problems, and poor drainage systems affecting neighborhood sanitation.

🛣️

Road Surface Damage

Potholes, cracks, uneven surfaces, road wear and tear that pose safety hazards to vehicles and pedestrians.

💡

Lighting & Visibility Issues

Broken streetlights, poor illumination, malfunctioning signals affecting safety and visibility in public areas.

🌿

Vegetation Overgrowth

Overgrown trees, bushes blocking pathways, unmaintained green spaces requiring landscaping attention.

🏗️

Structural Damage

Damaged sidewalks, broken curbs, deteriorating public infrastructure requiring immediate repair.

🗑️

Waste Management

Overflowing bins, illegal dumping, litter accumulation, and inadequate waste collection services.

🎨

Vandalism & Graffiti

Graffiti, property defacement, vandalized public property requiring cleanup and restoration.

🚧

Traffic & Signage

Missing signs, damaged traffic signals, faded road markings affecting traffic flow and safety.

🏞️

Public Amenities

Broken benches, damaged playground equipment, malfunctioning public facilities needing maintenance.

⚠️

Safety Hazards

Any immediate danger to public safety including exposed wires, unstable structures, or hazardous conditions.

🚰

Water Quality Issues

Contaminated water sources, discolored water supply, water quality concerns affecting public health.

🔊

Noise Pollution

Excessive noise from construction, traffic, or industrial activities disrupting residential peace.

🐕

Animal Control

Stray animals, pet waste issues, wildlife intrusion requiring animal control intervention.

🏚️

Abandoned Properties

Vacant buildings, abandoned structures, unmaintained properties affecting neighborhood safety and aesthetics.

🌫️

Air Quality

Smoke, dust, odors, and air pollution affecting respiratory health and quality of life.
