What is Neighborhood Issue Classifier?

🎯 The Problem

Residents often encounter various neighborhood issues like potholes, broken streetlights, overflowing trash bins, and graffiti. However, they struggle to identify the right department or platform to report these problems. As a result, many reports get lost in bureaucracy, delaying crucial repairs and maintenance.

  • ❌ Confusion about which department to contact
  • ❌ Reports getting lost in the system
  • ❌ Delayed response times
  • ❌ Lack of accountability
  • ❌ No priority system for urgent issues

💡 Our Solution

UrbanEye's intelligent platform empowers citizens to simply upload a photo of any neighborhood issue. Our advanced AI automatically categorizes the problem, assigns severity scores, and instantly sends it to the appropriate authority dashboard for immediate action.

  • ✅ Instant AI-powered categorization
  • ✅ Automatic routing to correct authority
  • ✅ Smart scoring system for prioritization
  • ✅ Faster response and resolution
  • ✅ Complete transparency and tracking
