How Our AI Model Works

Understanding the UrbanEye intelligent scoring and routing system

1

📸 Capture the Issue

Simply take a photo of the neighborhood issue using your smartphone camera through our Camera Detection page.

2

🤖 AI Image Analysis

Our advanced AI model analyzes the uploaded image to identify and classify the type of issue. The model is trained on thousands of neighborhood problem images to ensure accurate detection.

3

📊 Category-Based Scoring

The AI assigns a severity score to each detected category. Multiple issues can be identified in a single image, and each category receives its own score based on:

  • Severity of the damage or hazard
  • Potential safety risks
  • Impact on community well-being
  • Urgency of required intervention
4

🎯 Total Score Calculation

After analyzing all detected issues in the image, the system calculates a total severity score. This comprehensive score represents the overall urgency and priority of the reported problem.

Example:

Road Surface Damage Score: -40

Efficient Drainage Score: +30

Total Score: -10/140

5

⚡ Smart Authority Routing

Based on the total score and identified categories, the system automatically determines the next action:

🚨 High Priority (Score < 40)

Immediately routes to emergency services and responsible authorities with urgent notification

⚠️ Medium Priority (Score 40-69)

Routes to appropriate department with standard processing timeline

📋 Low Priority (Score ≥ 70)

Added to maintenance queue for scheduled resolution

6

📞 Authority Contact Suggestion

If the total score falls below the automatic routing threshold, the system suggests that you contact the respective authorities directly. You'll receive:

  • Contact information for the responsible department
  • Reference number for your report
  • Recommended next steps
  • Estimated response timeline

⚙️ About Our AI Model

Our AI Draft model continuously learns and improves its accuracy. Currently, the model specializes in detecting the most common neighborhood issues. While we've listed 15+ issue categories on our platform, the AI model is trained on the 10 categories that represent the majority of reported problems. This focused approach ensures high accuracy and reliable scoring for the issues that matter most to your community. Our AI Draft Model also doesn't currently contact the respective authorities directly. It is just our idea for future steps.
